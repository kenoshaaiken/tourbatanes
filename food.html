Food & Delicacies

Taste the unique flavors of Ivatan cooking — from sea to table.

The food of Batanes is shaped by the sea, the mountains, and centuries of Ivatan tradition.

Must-Try Ivatan Dishes

Local Drinks

Palek

Traditional Ivatan rice wine, brewed locally and served at community gatherings and celebrations.

Where to try: Local households and some guesthouses

Batanes Coffee

Locally grown coffee with a strong, earthy flavor. Most famously sold at the Honesty Coffee Shop in Ivana.

Where to try: Honesty Coffee Shop, Ivana

Fresh Coconut Juice

Refreshing and sweet, fresh coconut juice from Batanes' native trees is the perfect drink after a day of sightseeing.

Where to try: Local markets and roadside stalls

Where to Eat in Batanes

Most accommodations in Batanes offer home-cooked meals as part of their package. Local carinderias and small restaurants in Basco also serve authentic Ivatan food. Eating at these local spots is much better than big restaurants — and it helps support the community.
